How do I actually do this?

iPhone or iPad
  1. Tap the iPhone, iPad or Mac button above. iOS will ask if you want to subscribe to the calendar.
  2. Tap Subscribe, then Add.
  3. If nothing happens, copy the feed link and go to Settings → Apps → Calendar → Accounts → Add Account → Other → Add Subscribed Calendar, then paste it.
Android
  1. Android's Google Calendar app cannot add a subscription directly — you have to do it once from a browser.
  2. Tap the Google Calendar button above (a desktop browser is easiest), sign in and confirm Add calendar.
  3. Open the Google Calendar app on your phone, go to Settings, and turn the new calendar's Sync switch on.
Google Calendar on the web
  1. Tap the Google Calendar button, or in Google Calendar go to Other calendars → + → From URL.
  2. Paste the feed link and click Add calendar.
  3. Google refreshes subscribed calendars on its own schedule, sometimes only once every 12–24 hours. That is Google's behaviour, not a problem with the feed.
Outlook
  1. Tap the Outlook button above, or in Outlook on the web go to Add calendar → Subscribe from web.
  2. Paste the feed link, give it a name, and click Import.
  3. In Outlook desktop: File → Account Settings → Internet Calendars → New.
Something else (Fastmail, Proton, Thunderbird, Notion…)
  1. Copy the feed link with the Copy feed link button.
  2. Look for "subscribe by URL", "add from internet", or "ICS URL" in your app.
  3. Paste it. Anything that speaks iCalendar will work — these are plain RFC 5545 feeds.
